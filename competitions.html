🎰 Weekend Triple

Select competitors and draw three tables. For each table, first earns points equal to the number of competitors, descending to one point for last. Ties split the occupied finishing points. Overall ties break by most first-place finishes, then highest total raw score.

Select competitors. Each round draws a random table. The lowest score receives one strike. Three strikes eliminates a player. Continue until one player remains.
